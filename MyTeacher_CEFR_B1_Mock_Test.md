# MyTeacher CEFR — B1 Multilevel Namunaviy Mock Test (Pilot)
## Kontent metodisti va dasturchilar uchun ishlab chiqarish namunasi

**Daraja:** B1 (Intermediate / Threshold)
**Format:** Milliy Sertifikat / Multilevel (Bilim va malakalarni baholash agentligi uslubi)
**Versiya:** v1.0 · Pilot
**Maqsad:** Bitta to'liq B1 mock test namunasi. Metodist kontent sifatini, dasturchi ma'lumot tuzilmasini (9-ilova, JSON) shu asosda ko'radi.

> **Eslatma (halollik):** Bu test — real imtihon uslubiga mos **namunaviy** kontent. Standart ball → CEFR band chegaralari har sessiyada ekvatlanadi, shuning uchun 7-bo'limdagi mapping **taxminiy/illyustrativ**. Audio bo'limlar uchun transkriptlar berilgan (TTS yoki inson ovozi bilan yozib olinadi).
>
> **B1 xarakteri:** kundalik, tanish mavzular (oila, sayohat, ish, xarid, ta'lim); asosiy zamonlar (Present/Past Simple, Present Perfect, going to/will), aniq va sodda matnlar. Produktiv skill-lar (Speaking/Writing) hajmi B2'ga qaraganda kichikroq.

## 0. Test xaritasi va ballar

| # | Bo'lim | Topshiriq turlari | Savol | Vaqt | Xom ball |
|---|--------|-------------------|-------|------|----------|
| 1 | **Listening** | MCQ, Note completion, Matching | 30 | ~30 daq | 30 |
| 2 | **Reading** | Matching heading, T/F/NG, MCQ | 30 | ~60 daq | 30 |
| 3 | **Use of English** | MC cloze, Open cloze, Word formation, Key word | 30 | ~30 daq | 30 |

> Pilotda har obyektiv bo'lim uchun **10 ta namunaviy item** to'liq berilgan (real testda ~30 gacha kengaytiriladi).

## 1. LISTENING

### Part 1 — Short conversations (MCQ)
*Har suhbat bir marta yangraydi. To'g'ri javobni (A/B/C) tanlang.*

**Q1.** [Audio]
> **M:** Sorry I'm late! The bus didn't come on time, so in the end I had to walk.
> **W:** No problem. How long did it take you?
> **M:** Almost half an hour.

**Why was the man late?**
A. He missed the bus  B. The bus did not arrive ✅  C. He wanted to walk

**Q2.** [Audio]
> **W:** Are you free on Saturday? We're going to the cinema.
> **M:** I'd love to, but I'm working until six. Maybe the late show?

**What is the problem?**
A. He doesn't like the cinema  B. He has to work ✅  C. He has no money

**Q3.** [Audio]
> **W:** Excuse me, where's the nearest pharmacy?
> **M:** Go straight on, then turn left at the traffic lights. It's next to the bank.

**Where is the pharmacy?**
A. Next to the bank ✅  B. Opposite the bank  C. Behind the lights

**Q4.** [Audio]
> **M:** How was your weekend?
> **W:** Great! We visited my grandparents in the village. The weather was lovely.

**What did the woman do at the weekend?**
A. Stayed at home  B. Visited family ✅  C. Went shopping

### Part 2 — Monologue / Note completion
*Ma'ruzachini tinglang va bo'sh joylarni (Q5–Q9) BIR yoki IKKI so'z / raqam bilan to'ldiring.*

> **Transcript:** "Welcome to City Library. Let me give you some information. We are open from **nine** in the morning until eight in the evening, Monday to Friday. On Saturdays we close at **two o'clock**. To borrow books, you need a library **card**, which is free for students. You can keep each book for **three weeks**. If you return a book late, there is a small fine of **twenty** som per day. Our quiet study room is on the first floor."

| # | Notes | Answer |
|---|-------|--------|
| Q5 | The library opens at ________ a.m. | nine / 9 |
| Q6 | On Saturdays it closes at ________. | two o'clock / 2 |
| Q7 | To borrow books you need a library ________. | card |
| Q8 | You can keep a book for ________. | three weeks |
| Q9 | The fine for a late book is ________ som per day. | twenty / 20 |

### Part 3 — Matching (speaker ↔ opinion)
*Uch kishi bo'sh vaqti haqida gapiradi. Q10 uchun to'g'ri shaxsni tanlang.*

> **Olim:** I spend most of my free time playing football with friends.
> **Nodira:** I prefer staying at home and reading a good book.
> **Sardor:** For me, nothing beats cooking something new at the weekend.

**Q10. Who likes quiet activities at home?**
A. Olim  B. Nodira ✅  C. Sardor

**Listening answer key:** 1-B, 2-B, 3-A, 4-B, 5-nine, 6-two o'clock, 7-card, 8-three weeks, 9-twenty, 10-B

## 2. READING

### Text 1 — Matching heading
*A–D paragraflar. Har biriga (Q11–Q14) mos sarlavhani tanlang. Bitta sarlavha ORTIQCHA.*

**Headings:** i) The benefits of cycling  ii) A problem in big cities  iii) A cheap way to travel  iv) Learning to ride  v) The history of the bicycle *(distractor)*

> **A.** More and more people in our city ride bicycles to work. It is cheap, it keeps you fit, and it does not pollute the air. *(→ i)*
>
> **B.** Every morning, thousands of cars fill the roads. Traffic jams make journeys long and stressful for everyone. *(→ ii)*
>
> **C.** A bus ticket costs money every day, but once you buy a bicycle, riding it is almost free. *(→ iii)*
>
> **D.** Most children learn to ride when they are young. With a little practice, it soon becomes easy. *(→ iv)*

| # | Para | Answer |
|---|------|--------|
| Q11 | A | i |
| Q12 | B | ii |
| Q13 | C | iii |
| Q14 | D | iv |

### Text 2 — MCQ + True / False / Not Given
> **The City Museum.** The City Museum is a great place to visit at the weekend. It is open every day except Monday. Entrance is free for children under twelve, while adults pay a small fee at the door. Inside, you can see old photographs, traditional clothes, and objects from daily life a hundred years ago. There is also a small café that sells tea and cakes. The museum is closed on public holidays.

**Q15 (MCQ).** When is the museum closed?
A. Every weekend  B. On Mondays ✅  C. In summer

**Q16 (MCQ).** What can you buy in the museum café?
A. Books  B. Tickets  C. Tea and cakes ✅

*True / False / Not Given:*
**Q17.** Children under twelve pay to enter the museum. → **False**
**Q18.** The museum has photographs from the past. → **True**
**Q19.** The museum is the oldest building in the city. → **Not Given**

### Text 3 — Gapped text (sentence insertion)
*Matndan ikkita jumla olib tashlangan. Q20–Q21 uchun mos jumlani (a–c) tanlang. Bitta jumla ortiqcha.*

> Learning to cook is a useful skill for everyone. **(Q20) ____** You also save money, because eating at home is cheaper than eating in restaurants. **(Q21) ____** So why not start with a simple dish today?

**Options:**
a) When you cook yourself, you know exactly what is in your food. *(Q20)*
b) Cooking is also a fun way to relax after a busy day. *(Q21)*
c) Most restaurants in the city are open until midnight. *(distractor)*

| # | Answer |
|---|--------|
| Q20 | a |
| Q21 | b |

**Reading answer key:** 11-i, 12-ii, 13-iii, 14-iv, 15-B, 16-C, 17-False, 18-True, 19-Not Given, 20-a, 21-b

## 3. USE OF ENGLISH (Lexico-grammatical)

### Task A — Multiple-choice cloze (Q22–Q25)
> My sister is really good **(Q22)** playing the guitar. She **(Q23)** learning three years ago and now she plays in a band. Last summer they **(Q24)** at a big festival. I am very **(Q25)** of her.

| # | A | B | C | Answer |
|---|---|---|---|--------|
| Q22 | at | in | on | **A (good at)** |
| Q23 | started | stopped | finished | **A (started)** |
| Q24 | play | played | playing | **B (played)** |
| Q25 | proud | happy | interested | **A (proud of)** |

### Task B — Open cloze (Q26–Q28) — bo'sh joyga BIR so'z
> I have lived in this city **(Q26)** ____ ten years. I like it very much, **(Q27)** ____ sometimes the traffic is bad. Next year I am going **(Q28)** ____ move to a quieter area.

| # | Answer |
|---|--------|
| Q26 | for |
| Q27 | but |
| Q28 | to |

### Task C — Word formation (Q29–Q31)
> The trip was very **(Q29: ENJOY)** ____. Our guide was **(Q30: FRIEND)** ____ and gave us a lot of **(Q31: USE)** ____ information.

| # | Root | Answer |
|---|------|--------|
| Q29 | ENJOY | enjoyable |
| Q30 | FRIEND | friendly |
| Q31 | USE | useful |

### Task D — Key word transformation (Q32–Q34)
*Ikkinchi jumlani berilgan so'z bilan, ma'nosini o'zgartirmasdan tugating (2–4 so'z).*

**Q32.** This is my first visit to Paris. **(BEEN)**
> I have never **__________** Paris before.
> ✅ *been to*

**Q33.** "Let's go to the beach," said Ali. **(SUGGESTED)**
> Ali **__________** to the beach.
> ✅ *suggested going*

**Q34.** The film was so boring that we left. **(SUCH)**
> It was **__________** film that we left.
> ✅ *such a boring*

**Use of English answer key:** 22-A, 23-A, 24-B, 25-A, 26-for, 27-but, 28-to, 29-enjoyable, 30-friendly, 31-useful, 32-been to, 33-suggested going, 34-such a boring

## 4. WRITING (2 topshiriq · 30 ball · rubrika)

### Task 1 — Informal email (~80–100 so'z)
**Prompt:**
> A friend has invited you to visit their city. Write an email to your friend. In your email:
> - thank your friend for the invitation;
> - say when you can come;
> - ask one question about the visit.

**Model answer (B1, ~85 so'z):**
> Hi Aziz,
>
> Thank you so much for inviting me to visit your city! I'm really excited to see you.
>
> I have exams until the 15th, so I can come at the end of the month. I think I'll take the morning train and stay for the weekend.
>
> One question: what's the weather like there now? I want to know what clothes to bring.
>
> Please let me know if that works for you. I can't wait!
>
> See you soon,
> Sardor

**Baholash rubrikasi (Task 1 — 15 ball):**
| Kriteriya | Ball | B1 kutilma |
|---|---|---|
| Task achievement | 0–4 | Uch bandning barchasi yoritilgan, norasmiy uslub |
| Coherence & cohesion | 0–4 | Sodda bog'lovchilar (and, but, so, because) to'g'ri |
| Grammar range & accuracy | 0–4 | Asosiy zamonlar, umumiy aniqlik |
| Lexical resource | 0–3 | Kundalik leksika, aniq |

### Task 2 — Short opinion essay (~100–120 so'z)
**Prompt:**
> Some people prefer to study in the morning, while others prefer to study in the evening. Which do you prefer, and why?

**Model answer (B1, ~110 so'z):**
> Everyone has their own favourite time to study. Some people like the morning, and others like the evening. In my opinion, the morning is better.
>
> First, in the morning my mind is fresh after sleeping. I can understand new things more easily and I do not feel tired. Second, when I study early, I have free time later to relax or meet my friends.
>
> However, some people say the evening is quieter, because everyone is at home. This is true, but I often feel sleepy at night.
>
> For these reasons, I prefer studying in the morning. It works best for me.

**Baholash rubrikasi (Task 2 — 15 ball):**
| Kriteriya | Ball | B1 kutilma |
|---|---|---|
| Task achievement | 0–4 | Aniq fikr + sabablar, kirish/xulosa bor |
| Coherence & cohesion | 0–4 | Paragraf tuzilmasi, first/second/however |
| Grammar range & accuracy | 0–4 | Turli sodda tuzilma, aniqlik |
| Lexical resource | 0–3 | Mavzuga mos leksika |

## 5. SPEAKING (3 qism · ~12 daq · 30 ball · rubrika)

### Part 1 — Interview (~3 daq)
Ekspert tanish mavzularda savol beradi:
- **Yourself:** *Can you tell me a little about yourself? What do you do?*
- **Free time:** *What do you usually do at the weekend? Do you have a hobby?*
- **Daily life:** *How do you usually travel to work or school?*

### Part 2 — Picture description / short talk (~4 daq)
> **Cue:** Talk about your home town for about a minute. Say **where it is, what you like about it, and one thing you would change.**
>
> Follow-up: *Would you like to live somewhere else in the future? Why / why not?*

**Kutiladigan til (B1):** sodda tasvir (*there is / there are*), yoqtirish (*I like… because…*), kelasi/shart (*I would…*).

### Part 3 — Short discussion (~4 daq)
Tanish mavzuda oddiy muhokama:
- *Is it better to live in a city or in a village?*
- *Do you think young people spend too much time on their phones?*
- *How important is it to learn foreign languages?*

### Baholash rubrikasi (Speaking — 30 ball, 5 kriteriya × 6)
| Kriteriya | B1 kutilma |
|---|---|
| **Fluency & coherence** | Sodda gaplar ravon, ba'zi pauzalar tabiiy |
| **Grammar range & accuracy** | Asosiy zamonlar, umumiy tushunarli |
| **Lexical resource** | Kundalik mavzu leksikasi yetarli |
| **Pronunciation** | Umuman tushunarli |
| **Interaction** | Savolni tushunadi, qisqa javob beradi va rivojlantiradi |

## 7. Baholash yo'riqnomasi

1. **Obyektiv bo'limlar (L/R/UoE):** har to'g'ri javob = 1 xom ball. Pilotda 10 item/bo'lim; real testda 30 item → 30 ball.
2. **Rasch → standart ball:** xom ball item difficulty bilan tortiladi va **0–75** shkalaga keltiriladi (backend: kalibrlangan item bank).
3. **Writing/Speaking:** har kriteriya AI + ekspert tomonidan baholanadi, o'rtacha olinadi.
4. **Yakuniy:** 5 bo'lim → umumiy standart ball → CEFR band. **O'tish = ≥ 60%.**

### Taxminiy band mapping (illyustrativ)
| Standart ball (0–75) | Taxminiy band |
|---|---|
| < 30 | A2 dan past |
| 30–44 | **B1** |
| 45–59 | B2 |
| 60–75 | C1 |

> B1 mock uchun asosiy maqsad — **B1 chegarasini (≥60%)** tasdiqlash. Yuqori ball B2 potentsialini ko'rsatadi.

---

## 8. Diagnostic vs Full mock (mahsulotga tatbiq)

- **Diagnostic mock (~10 daq):** har bo'limdan 3–4 item — darajani tez baholaydi.
- **Full mock (~3 soat):** to'liq, timed, 5 bo'lim ketma-ket.
- Zaif bo'lim (mas. Q32–34 xato → Key word) avtomatik **SRS + fokus lesson**ga qo'shiladi.
