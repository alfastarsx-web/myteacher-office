# MyTeacher CEFR — C1 Multilevel Namunaviy Mock Test (Pilot)
## Kontent metodisti va dasturchilar uchun ishlab chiqarish namunasi

**Daraja:** C1 (Advanced / Effective Operational Proficiency)
**Format:** Milliy Sertifikat / Multilevel (Bilim va malakalarni baholash agentligi uslubi)
**Versiya:** v1.0 · Pilot
**Maqsad:** Bitta to'liq C1 mock test namunasi. Metodist kontent sifatini, dasturchi ma'lumot tuzilmasini (9-ilova, JSON) shu asosda ko'radi.

> **Eslatma (halollik):** Bu test — real imtihon uslubiga mos **namunaviy** kontent. Standart ball → CEFR band chegaralari har sessiyada ekvatlanadi; 7-bo'limdagi mapping **taxminiy/illyustrativ**. Audio bo'limlar uchun transkriptlar berilgan.
>
> **C1 xarakteri:** abstrakt, akademik va professional mavzular (iqtisod, texnologiya, ijtimoiy masalalar); murakkab grammatika (inversion, cleft, passive reporting, modals of deduction, nuanced connectors); implicit ma'no, muallif munosabati va nozik farqlar. Produktiv skill-lar (Speaking/Writing) hajmi va talablari eng yuqori.

## 2. READING

### Text 1 — Matching headings
*A–E paragraflar. Har biriga (Q11–Q15) mos sarlavhani tanlang. Bitta sarlavha ORTIQCHA.*

**Headings:** i) A tradition that has changed its function  ii) An objection and a response  iii) The limits of a popular measure  iv) A shift driven by necessity  v) An unintended consequence  vi) A purely aesthetic concern *(distractor)*

> **A.** Far from being a modern invention, the custom can be traced back centuries, though its purpose has shifted gradually from the ceremonial to the commercial. *(→ i)*
>
> **B.** GDP remains the headline figure, yet it captures neither the distribution of wealth nor the depletion of natural resources on which future prosperity depends. *(→ iii)*
>
> **C.** Critics contend that such schemes merely reward the wealthy; proponents reply that, without them, investment would dry up entirely. *(→ ii)*
>
> **D.** When traditional industries collapsed, communities had little choice but to reinvent themselves, often turning to tourism and services. *(→ iv)*
>
> **E.** Ironically, the very policy designed to reduce congestion encouraged more people to move to the outskirts, lengthening their commutes. *(→ v)*

| # | Para | Answer |
|---|------|--------|
| Q11 | A | i |
| Q12 | B | iii |
| Q13 | C | ii |
| Q14 | D | iv |
| Q15 | E | v |

### Text 2 — MCQ (inference) + True / False / Not Given
> **The Automation Debate.** In discussions of automation, alarm tends to drown out nuance. The author concedes that machines will undoubtedly eliminate certain roles — as every previous technological revolution has done. Yet history offers grounds for cautious optimism: technology has, on balance, created more jobs than it has destroyed, even if the transition has often been painful for those caught in it. The crucial question, then, is not whether jobs will disappear, but whether societies will invest in retraining quickly enough to soften the blow.

A. Uniformly pessimistic about automation

**Q17 (MCQ).** According to the author, what is the "crucial question"?
A. Whether machines are affordable

## 3. USE OF ENGLISH (Lexico-grammatical)

### Task A — Multiple-choice cloze (Q24–Q27)
> The new regulations came **(Q24)** heavy criticism from independent experts, who argued they had been rushed through **(Q25)** proper consultation. Ministers, for their part, **(Q26)** the objections aside, insisting that delay was **(Q27)** the question.

| # | A | B | C | D | Answer |
|---|---|---|---|---|--------|
| Q24 | under | over | across | into | **A (came under)** |
| Q25 | without | despite | unless | besides | **A (without)** |
| Q26 | brushed | swept | wiped | pushed | **A (brushed aside)** |

### Task D — Key word transformation (Q34–Q36)
*Ikkinchi jumlani berilgan so'z bilan, ma'nosini o'zgartirmasdan tugating (3–6 so'z).*

**Q34.** People say he is the best surgeon in the country. **(SAID)**
> He **__________** the best surgeon in the country.
> ✅ *is said to be*

**Q35.** I regret not studying harder at school. **(WISH)**
> I **__________** harder at school.
> ✅ *wish I had studied*

**Q36.** They will probably cancel the event. **(LIKELY)**
> The event **__________** cancelled.
> ✅ *is likely to be*

**Use of English answer key:** 24-A, 25-A, 26-A, 27-A, 28-has, 29-least, 30-whether, 31-reliance, 32-rigorous, 33-assumptions, 34-is said to be, 35-wish I had studied, 36-is likely to be

## 4. WRITING (2 topshiriq · 30 ball · rubrika)

### Task 1 — Formal letter / proposal (~150–180 so'z)
**Prompt:**
> You read an article in an international magazine arguing that studying abroad is a waste of money for most students. Write a formal letter to the editor responding to the article. In your letter, challenge the author's main claim, provide two counter-arguments, and propose a more balanced view.

**Model answer (C1, ~165 so'z):**
> Dear Editor,
>
> I am writing in response to last week's article, "The Overrated Overseas Degree," whose central claim — that studying abroad rarely justifies its cost — strikes me as both sweeping and misleading.
>
> First, the author reduces the value of an international education to future salary alone. This overlooks its less tangible returns: the independence, adaptability and cross-cultural fluency that employers increasingly prize. Second, the argument treats all students as identical. For those from countries with limited academic infrastructure, an overseas degree may offer opportunities simply unavailable at home.
>
> None of this is to deny that studying abroad can be prohibitively expensive, or that it is not right for everyone. A fairer conclusion would acknowledge the trade-offs and encourage prospective students to weigh them carefully, rather than dismissing the option outright.
>
> I trust you will consider publishing a more nuanced follow-up.
>
> Yours faithfully,
> S. Xudoyberdiyev

**Baholash rubrikasi (Task 1 — 15 ball):**
| Kriteriya | Ball | C1 kutilma |
|---|---|---|
| Task achievement | 0–4 | Da'voni rad etadi, 2 kontr-argument, muvozanatli xulosa; rasmiy registr |
| Coherence & cohesion | 0–4 | Ravon, murakkab bog'lanish; g'oyalar mantiqiy zanjiri |
| Grammar range & accuracy | 0–4 | Keng grammatik doira (inversion, nominalisation), yuqori aniqlik |
| Lexical resource | 0–3 | Aniq, idiomatik, registrga mos leksika |

### Task 2 — Discursive essay (~220–260 so'z)
**Prompt:**
> "Governments should prioritise environmental protection over economic growth." To what extent do you agree? Justify your position with clear arguments and examples.

**Model answer (C1, ~240 so'z) — qisqartirilgan tuzilma:**
> The tension between environmental protection and economic growth is among the defining dilemmas of our age. While I broadly agree that governments must give the environment greater priority, framing the two as opposites is, I would argue, a false dichotomy.
>
> Those who favour growth above all point to jobs and rising living standards, particularly in developing economies where poverty remains acute. There is force in this: no government can ignore the immediate welfare of its citizens. Yet such growth, if pursued heedlessly, undermines the very resources on which long-term prosperity depends. Depleted soils, polluted air and a destabilised climate impose costs that no quarterly GDP figure captures.
>
> The more persuasive position, therefore, is not to sacrifice one for the other but to redefine growth itself. Investment in renewable energy, sustainable agriculture and green infrastructure can generate employment while safeguarding the environment — as several economies have already begun to demonstrate.
>
> Admittedly, the transition carries short-term costs, and these fall unevenly across society. Governments must cushion them through retraining and targeted support, or risk a public backlash that stalls reform altogether.
>
> In conclusion, environmental protection deserves priority, but the goal should be to reconcile it with prosperity rather than to treat the two as irreconcilable. A wiser conception of growth serves both.

**Baholash rubrikasi (Task 2 — 15 ball):**
| Kriteriya | Ball | C1 kutilma |
|---|---|---|
| Task achievement | 0–4 | Aniq pozitsiya, chuqur argumentlar + misollar, qarshi fikr tan olinadi |
| Coherence & cohesion | 0–4 | Yaxlit tuzilma, nozik o'tishlar, mantiqiy rivoj |
| Grammar range & accuracy | 0–4 | Murakkab tuzilmalar erkin, deyarli xatosiz |
| Lexical resource | 0–3 | Boy, aniq, akademik leksika va kollokatsiyalar |

## 5. SPEAKING (3 qism · ~18 daq · 30 ball · rubrika)

### Part 1 — Interview (~4 daq)
Shaxsiy, lekin fikr talab qiladigan savollar:
- *How have your goals changed over the past few years?*
- *What role does technology play in your daily work or study?*
- *Is there something you have changed your mind about recently?*

### Part 2 — Long turn / abstract prompt (~5 daq)
> **Cue:** Talk for two minutes about the following statement: **"Individual choices matter little in solving global problems."** Explain to what extent you agree, giving reasons and examples.
>
> Follow-up: *Where should responsibility mainly lie — with individuals, governments, or corporations?*

**Kutiladigan til (C1):** murakkab argumentatsiya (*while it is true that…, one might counter that…*), gedjing (*arguably, to some degree*), abstraksiya, aniq kollokatsiya.

### Part 3 — Abstract discussion (~6 daq)
Chuqur, ikki tomonlama muhokama:
- *To what extent is individual action effective against problems like climate change?*
- *Should short-term economic interests ever override long-term sustainability?*
- *How far can technology be trusted to solve the problems it helps create?*

### Baholash rubrikasi (Speaking — 30 ball, 5 kriteriya × 6)
| Kriteriya | C1 kutilma |
|---|---|
| **Fluency & coherence** | Uzun, ravon nutq; g'oyalar murakkab bog'lanadi, kam tortinish |
| **Grammar range & accuracy** | Keng va murakkab tuzilmalar, izchil aniqlik |
| **Lexical resource** | Aniq, idiomatik, abstrakt mavzuga yetarli boy leksika |
| **Pronunciation** | Tabiiy intonatsiya va urg'u, to'liq tushunarli |
| **Interaction** | Muhokamani boshqaradi, fikrni asoslaydi va rivojlantiradi |

## 7. Baholash yo'riqnomasi

1. **Obyektiv bo'limlar (L/R/UoE):** har to'g'ri javob = 1 xom ball. Pilotda 10 item/bo'lim; real testda 30 item → 30 ball.
2. **Rasch → standart ball:** xom ball item difficulty bilan tortiladi va **0–75** shkalaga keltiriladi. C1 itemlar odatda yuqori difficulty (musbat) qiymatga ega.
3. **Writing/Speaking:** har kriteriya AI + ekspert tomonidan baholanadi, o'rtacha olinadi.
4. **Yakuniy:** 5 bo'lim → umumiy standart ball → CEFR band. **O'tish = ≥ 60%.**

### Taxminiy band mapping (illyustrativ)
| Standart ball (0–75) | Taxminiy band |
|---|---|
| < 45 | B1 dan past |
| 45–59 | B2 |
| 60–75 | **C1** |

> C1 mock uchun asosiy maqsad — **C1 chegarasini (≥60%)** tasdiqlash. C1 bu multilevel testning yuqori shifti (C2 alohida sertifikatlanmaydi).
