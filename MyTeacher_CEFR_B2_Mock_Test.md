# MyTeacher CEFR — B2 Multilevel Namunaviy Mock Test (Pilot)
## Kontent metodisti va dasturchilar uchun ishlab chiqarish namunasi

**Daraja:** B2 (Upper-Intermediate)
**Format:** Milliy Sertifikat / Multilevel (Bilim va malakalarni baholash agentligi uslubi)
**Versiya:** v1.0 · Pilot
**Maqsad:** Bu hujjat — bitta to'liq mock test namunasi. Metodist kontent sifatini, dasturchi esa ma'lumot tuzilmasini (9-ilova, JSON) shu asosda ko'radi.

> **Eslatma (halollik):** Quyidagi test — real imtihon uslubiga mos **namunaviy** kontent. Standart ball → CEFR band chegaralari har sessiyada **ekvatlanadi** (Rasch item difficulty asosida), shuning uchun 8-bo'limdagi mapping **taxminiy/illyustrativ**. Audio bo'limlar uchun transkriptlar berilgan (TTS yoki inson ovozi bilan yozib olinadi).

## 0. Test xaritasi va ballar

| # | Bo'lim | Topshiriq turlari | Savol | Vaqt | Xom ball |
|---|--------|-------------------|-------|------|----------|
| 1 | **Listening** | MCQ, Note completion, Speaker matching | 30 | ~35 daq | 30 |
| 2 | **Reading** | Matching headings, T/F/NG, MCQ, Gapped text | 30 | ~70 daq | 30 |
| 3 | **Use of English** | MC cloze, Open cloze, Word formation, Key word | 30 | ~35 daq | 30 |
| 4 | **Writing** | Task 1 (email), Task 2 (essay) | 2 | ~50 daq | 30 |

- **Jami:** 150 ball · **O'tish:** ≥ 60%
- Listening / Reading / Use of English → obyektiv, avtomatik baho (Rasch → 0–75 standart ball)
- Writing / Speaking → rubrika (AI + ekspert), kriteriya bo'yicha o'rtacha

> Ushbu pilotda har bir obyektiv bo'lim uchun **10 ta namunaviy item** to'liq berilgan (real imtihonda ~30 gacha kengaytiriladi). Writing/Speaking to'liq (prompt + model + rubrika).

## 1. LISTENING

### Part 1 — Short conversations (MCQ)
*Har suhbat bir marta yangraydi. To'g'ri javobni (A/B/C) tanlang.*

**Q1.** [Audio transkript]
> **W:** Have you booked the meeting room for Thursday?
> **M:** I tried, but it was already taken. I put us down for Friday morning instead.
> **W:** That works, as long as it's before eleven.

**Where will the meeting take place first?**
A. Thursday afternoon  B. Friday morning ✅  C. Friday afternoon

**Q2.** [Audio]
> **M:** The train's delayed again — twenty minutes this time.
> **W:** Honestly, I'd rather take the bus from now on. At least it's predictable.

**How does the woman feel about the train?**
A. Confused  B. Frustrated ✅  C. Relieved

**Q3.** [Audio]
> **W:** Did you finish the report?
> **M:** Almost — I just need to add the figures from finance. They said they'd send them by five.

**What is the man waiting for?**
A. Approval from his manager  B. Data from another department ✅  C. Feedback on his writing

**Q4.** [Audio]
> **M:** I thought the film was a bit long, to be honest.
> **W:** Really? I could have watched another hour. The ending was worth it.

**What do the speakers disagree about?**
A. The film's length ✅  B. The ticket price  C. The main actor

### Part 2 — Monologue / Note completion
*Ma'ruzachini tinglang va bo'sh joylarni (Q5–Q9) BIR yoki IKKI so'z bilan to'ldiring.*

Photography is allowed everywhere except the **energy** exhibition, where the lighting is sensitive. If you'd like an audio guide, they're available at the front desk for a small **deposit**, which you get back at the end. Our café on the second floor serves lunch until **half past two**, and members receive a ten per cent discount. Finally, please keep your ticket, as you'll need it to re-enter after the **break**."

### Part 3 — Discussion / Speaker matching
*Uchta o'quvchi guruh loyihasi haqida gaplashadi. Har fikrni (Q10) tegishli shaxsga moslang.*

> **Anna:** I think we spent too long deciding roles. If we'd just started, we'd have finished sooner.
> **Bek:** For me, the hardest part was the presentation — I get nervous speaking in front of people.

## 2. READING

### Text 1 — Matching headings
*A–E paragraflar. Har biriga (Q11–Q15) mos sarlavhani ro'yxatdan tanlang. Bitta sarlavha ORTIQCHA.*

**Headings:** i) A change driven by cost  ii) An unexpected social benefit  iii) Resistance from older staff  iv) The role of technology  v) A trend that may not last  vi) Problems with productivity *(distractor turlari)*

> **A.** When companies first sent employees home during the pandemic, most saw it as temporary. Few imagined that, years later, the majority would still spend part of their week working remotely. *(→ v / trend)*
>
> **B.** For many businesses, the appeal was financial. Smaller offices meant lower rent, reduced energy bills and fewer overheads — savings that went straight to the bottom line. *(→ i / cost)*
>
> **C.** None of this would have been possible without reliable video calls, shared documents and fast home internet, which turned the kitchen table into a functioning workplace. *(→ iv / technology)*
>
> **D.** Unexpectedly, some employees reported stronger friendships. Freed from long commutes, they used the time saved to reconnect with family and neighbours. *(→ ii / social benefit)*
>
> **E.** Yet not everyone adapted easily. Several long-serving managers admitted they struggled to trust staff they could no longer see. *(→ iii / resistance)*

| # | Para | Answer |
|---|------|--------|
| Q11 | A | v |
| Q12 | B | i |
| Q13 | C | iv |
| Q14 | D | ii |
| Q15 | E | iii |

### Text 2 — MCQ + True / False / Not Given
> **The Return of the Night Train.** For decades, night trains seemed a relic of the past, pushed aside by cheap flights. Recently, however, they have made a striking comeback across Europe. Concerned about the environmental cost of aviation, a growing number of travellers now choose to sleep their way between capitals. New routes connect cities that had no direct link for years, and several operators report that tickets sell out weeks in advance. Critics argue the trains remain expensive compared with budget airlines, and that comfort varies widely. Supporters counter that arriving rested in a city centre, without airport queues, is worth the price.

**Q16 (MCQ).** Why have night trains become popular again?
A. They are cheaper than flights
B. Travellers worry about the environment ✅
C. Airports have closed
D. Flights have become slower

**Q17 (MCQ).** What do critics say about night trains?
A. They are too slow
B. They are often empty
C. They can be costly and inconsistent ✅
D. They only run in summer

*True / False / Not Given:*
**Q18.** Some night-train routes connect cities that previously had no direct service. → **True**
**Q19.** Night-train tickets are always cheaper than budget flights. → **False**
**Q20.** Most night-train passengers are business travellers. → **Not Given**

### Text 3 — Gapped text (sentence insertion)
*Matndan uchta jumla olib tashlangan. Q21–Q23 uchun mos jumlani (a–d) tanlang. Bitta jumla ortiqcha.*

> Learning a language as an adult is often said to be harder than as a child. **(Q21) ____** Adults, by contrast, can use grammar rules, dictionaries and their first language to make sense of new material. **(Q22) ____** What they often lack is not ability but time and regular exposure. **(Q23) ____** For this reason, short daily practice tends to beat occasional long sessions.

**Options:**
a) In fact, in some areas — such as vocabulary and understanding rules — adults can progress faster. *(Q22)*
b) This belief, however, is only partly true. *(Q21)*
c) A busy adult may study for an hour once a week, while a child hears the language for hours every day. *(Q23)*
d) Most schools now teach two foreign languages from an early age. *(distractor)*

| # | Answer |
|---|--------|
| Q21 | b |
| Q22 | a |
| Q23 | c |

**Reading answer key:** 11-v, 12-i, 13-iv, 14-ii, 15-iii, 16-B, 17-C, 18-True, 19-False, 20-Not Given, 21-b, 22-a, 23-c

## 3. USE OF ENGLISH (Lexico-grammatical)

### Task A — Multiple-choice cloze (Q24–Q27)
> Volunteering can be a powerful way to build new skills. Many people **(Q24)** up volunteering to help others, but soon discover they **(Q25)** just as much themselves. It looks good on a CV, and it can **(Q26)** to unexpected job offers. The key is to choose a cause you genuinely **(Q27)** about.

| # | A | B | C | D | Answer |
|---|---|---|---|---|--------|
| Q24 | take | pick | put | set | **A (take up)** |
| Q25 | win | gain | earn | reach | **B (gain)** |
| Q26 | lead | bring | cause | result | **A (lead to)** |
| Q27 | care | mind | worry | think | **A (care about)** |

### Task B — Open cloze (Q28–Q30) — bo'sh joyga BIR so'z

| # | Answer |
|---|--------|
| Q28 | the |
| Q29 | for |
| Q30 | than |

| # | Root | Answer |

### Task D — Key word transformation (Q34–Q36)
*Ikkinchi jumlani berilgan so'z bilan, ma'nosini o'zgartirmasdan tugating (2–5 so'z).*

**Q34.** They cancelled the concert because of the storm. **(OFF)**
> The concert **__________** because of the storm.
> ✅ *was called off*

**Q35.** "I'll definitely help you," she said. **(PROMISED)**
> She **__________** me.
> ✅ *promised to help*

**Q36.** It isn't necessary to book in advance. **(HAVE)**
> You **__________** book in advance.
> ✅ *don't have to*

**Use of English answer key:** 24-A, 25-B, 26-A, 27-A, 28-the, 29-for, 30-than, 31-success, 32-creative, 33-informative, 34-was called off, 35-promised to help, 36-don't have to

## 4. WRITING (2 topshiriq · 30 ball · rubrika bo'yicha)

### Task 1 — Semi-formal email (~120–150 so'z)
**Prompt:**
> You recently attended an online course with *BrightSkills Academy*, but you experienced technical problems during the lessons. Write an email to the course organiser. In your email:
> - explain which problems you had;
> - describe how they affected your learning;
> - say what you would like the academy to do.

**Model answer (B2, ~135 so'z):**
> Dear Sir or Madam,
>
> I am writing regarding the "Digital Marketing" course I completed last month. While the content was excellent, I faced repeated technical difficulties that affected my experience.
>
> During several live sessions, the video kept freezing, and on two occasions the platform logged me out completely. As a result, I missed important explanations and had to rely on other students' notes to catch up.
>
> I would be grateful if you could either provide recordings of the affected lessons or offer access to a future session at no extra cost. I believe this would be a fair solution given the circumstances.
>
> Thank you for your attention. I look forward to your reply.
>
> Yours faithfully,
> Sardor X.

**Baholash rubrikasi (Task 1 — 15 ball):**
| Kriteriya | Ball | B2 kutilma |
|---|---|---|
| Task achievement | 0–4 | Uch bandning barchasi yoritilgan, uslub (semi-formal) mos |
| Coherence & cohesion | 0–4 | Paragraflar mantiqiy, bog'lovchilar to'g'ri |
| Grammar range & accuracy | 0–4 | Murakkab tuzilmalar, kam xato |
| Lexical resource | 0–3 | Vaziyatga mos leksika, takror emas |

### Task 2 — Opinion essay (~180–220 so'z)
**Prompt:**
> Some people believe that students should use their smartphones in class for learning. Others think phones are a distraction and should be banned. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

**Model answer (B2, ~200 so'z) — qisqartirilgan tuzilma:**
> Smartphones are now part of everyday life, and their place in the classroom is widely debated. While some see them as valuable learning tools, others regard them as a serious distraction.
>
> On the one hand, phones give students instant access to dictionaries, educational apps and reliable information. A learner who does not understand a word can look it up in seconds, and interactive quizzes can make lessons more engaging than a textbook alone.
>
> On the other hand, the same device that helps can easily harm. Notifications from social media interrupt concentration, and it is difficult for teachers to check whether students are working or messaging friends. Studies suggest that even the presence of a phone can reduce focus.
>
> In my opinion, phones should be allowed but carefully controlled. Rather than banning them completely, schools could permit their use only for specific tasks, keeping them out of sight at other times.
>
> To conclude, smartphones are neither purely helpful nor purely harmful. With clear rules, their benefits can be enjoyed while their drawbacks are kept to a minimum.

**Baholash rubrikasi (Task 2 — 15 ball):**
| Kriteriya | Ball | B2 kutilma |
|---|---|---|
| Task achievement | 0–4 | Ikki qarash + o'z fikri, kirish/xulosa bor |
| Coherence & cohesion | 0–4 | Aniq paragraf tuzilmasi, ravon o'tishlar |
| Grammar range & accuracy | 0–4 | Turli zamon/tuzilma, aniqlik |
| Lexical resource | 0–3 | Akademik/argument leksikasi |

## 5. SPEAKING (3 qism · ~15 daq · 30 ball · rubrika)

### Part 1 — Interview (~3 daq)
Ekspert 2–3 mavzuda savol beradi:
- **Home town:** *Where are you from? What do you like most about living there?*
- **Work/Study:** *What are you studying? How useful will it be in the future?*
- **Free time:** *How do you usually spend your weekends? Has that changed recently?*

### Part 2 — Long turn / photo comparison (~4 daq)
> **Cue:** Compare the two photographs and say **why people might choose each way of studying** — studying alone at home vs. studying in a group at a library. You have one minute to talk.
>
> Follow-up: *Which do you prefer, and why?*

**Kutiladigan til (B2):** taqqoslash tuzilmalari (*whereas, while, both...*), taxmin (*they might..., it could be...*), sabab bog'lovchilari.

### Part 3 — Discussion (~5 daq)
Abstrakt savollar, fikr asoslash:
- *Do you think traditional classrooms will disappear in the future?*
- *What are the advantages and disadvantages of studying online?*
- *How important is it to learn together with other people?*

### Baholash rubrikasi (Speaking — 30 ball, 5 kriteriya × 6)
| Kriteriya | B2 kutilma |
|---|---|
| **Fluency & coherence** | Uzun gaplar, kam pauza, fikrni bog'laydi |
| **Grammar range & accuracy** | Murakkab tuzilmalar, umumiy aniqlik |
| **Lexical resource** | Mavzuga mos boy leksika, paraphrase |
| **Pronunciation** | Tushunarli, intonatsiya tabiiy |
| **Interaction** | Savolni tushunadi, muhokamani rivojlantiradi |

## 7. Baholash yo'riqnomasi

1. **Obyektiv bo'limlar (L/R/UoE):** har to'g'ri javob = 1 xom ball. Pilotda 10 item/bo'lim; real testda 30 item → 30 ball.
2. **Rasch → standart ball:** xom ball to'g'ridan-to'g'ri emas, **item difficulty** bilan tortiladi va **0–75** shkalaga keltiriladi (backend: kalibrlangan item bank kerak).
3. **Writing/Speaking:** har kriteriya AI + ekspert tomonidan baholanadi, o'rtacha olinadi.
4. **Yakuniy:** 5 bo'lim → umumiy standart ball → CEFR band. **O'tish = ≥ 60%.**

### Taxminiy band mapping (illyustrativ — real cut-score sessiyada ekvatlanadi)
| Standart ball (0–75) | Taxminiy band |
|---|---|
| < 30 | A2 dan past |
| 30–44 | B1 |
| 45–59 | **B2** |
| 60–75 | C1 |

## 8. Diagnostic vs Full mock (mahsulotga tatbiq)

- **Diagnostic mock (~15 daq):** har bo'limdan 3–5 item — foydalanuvchi darajasini tez baholaydi, "target plan" tuzadi.
- **Full mock (~3 soat):** to'liq, timed, 5 bo'lim ketma-ket — real imtihon bosimi.
- Natijada zaif bo'lim (mas. Q34–36 xato → Key word transformation) avtomatik **SRS + fokus lesson**ga qo'shiladi.
